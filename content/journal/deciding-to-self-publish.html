So at this stage, I am still sending out agent queries, yet I have decided to self publish, knowing that this doesn’t mean I can’t get an agent later. Right now, I’d like to get a book in my hands, and sell them to people I know, and get an ePUB version on Amazon and other places online. If sales are good, then an agent might be even more interested, so it’s worth it I think.

The next step was to choose the company that I was going to use. I read several comparisons, such as this one, but in the end I decided to go with Ingram Spark.
