I was happy to find out that the Courier Press ran an announcement for my book signing. I was actually unaware that they were doing it. Someone at work brought it to me and said “Hey, I see you made print today.” I had never heard it said that way. He’s not the youngest chicken in the coop. Anyway, it’s always cool to see your name in print. Plus it went out on Friday, which I was told is a good day for that sort of thing. I bought three copies to keep! Thanks again CP.
