So most books have typos, errors, and correction needs, especially the first edition or two. So since several people have told me they found errors in the book, I made this blog entry for people to comment on and leave me notes. That way I can make the corrections and then give them to the publisher. Please see if someone else already notified me about the one you found before leaving it in a comment below. Thanks.
