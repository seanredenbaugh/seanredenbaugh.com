--- title: Finished rough draft of my novel Sunlight Parted date: '2014-05-16' type: journal tags: - Writing ---

Well, 3 years after I outlined the book, I have completed the rough draft! I know it seems like a long time, but really I didn’t work too hard the first two years. To give you an idea of my progress, consider this – I outlined the book and wrote the first 5 chapters in 2 1/2 yrs, and completed the last 17 chapters between October 2013, and May 2014. So basically I wrote 75% of the book in 7-8 months. That really isn’t that bad.

The book details, along with a story synopsis, can be found on , but here is a quick synopsis – 21 chapters & 59,000 words. It is my first novel, and would fall into the genre of “Literary Fiction.”

Next step… Find a professional editor to get excited about my book and help me polish this manuscript.
