I have developed several ideas for a new book, many of which have been started already. But I have finally decided on the one that will be my next novel.

I am working on the outline now. It will be titled…


Salima Falls



…and will explore the themes of self forgiveness, moving on after tragedy, losing someone, finding someone new, healing old wounds, and allowing yourself to Love again.  And of course there is an element of the supernatural as well, with a sort of “magic” cab and a small town of questionable reality.

Just for fun, I’ve whipped up a simple cover that will probably change later on in the process.  A cool part about this is that I have received permission from the painter of the bottom image in the cover to use his artwork.  I will post his information inside the book when it is finally published.  That was our deal.  Oh… and he gets a free book.
