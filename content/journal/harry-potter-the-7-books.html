Well, I finally finished all the books! Someone convinced me about a month and a half ago that it was necessary for me to read the entire series. I had seen the first few movies at the time and I really liked them. So I bought the first two and read them. I got hooked and kept reading one after another. I read about 1 book a week it seemed until I just finished the last one today during lunch.

I had thought my favorite was The Prisoner of Azkaban until the last book. I think Deathly Hallows is now my favorite, although I could have done without the last “19 Years Later” chapter. I think it could have just ended right before that with him pulling Ginny away from the crowd and kissing her, confessing his Love, and then hinting at a future. I feel like she put too much effort into tying up every loose end. But either way, it was very good. The whole series was awesome. I know they were written for kids at first, but I never felt like I was reading a kids book. Not even the first one. Though by the end it was definitely not for children. The books grew up along with the readers.

I am not sure what I’m going to read next, but I think I’ll take a break from reading to get back to writing. I have a script to start, and another novel to finish. Wish me luck!
