I’m so excited to finally be holding a physical copy of my book! I really like the size I have chosen (5.25 x 8.00 inches), since that is my favorite size for a novel, but with only 61,000 words, it became 210 pages, and slightly thinner than I would have liked. Maybe I’ll get some mass market paperbacks printed someday if it’s ever needed.

Overall I think the quality of the book is very nice. The cover turned out great. The pages are a little too bright white for me, but I suppose that’s ok. High contrast.
