WOW!  I just completed Dan Brown’s The Lost Symbol and WOW is the quickest way to describe it.  When you finish the last page you will be astounded.  The last word even sticks with you.  I don’t know how he does it, but he’s amazing.  I thought this was even better than the DaVinci Code.  I can only dream to write like this.  Highly recommend it.  I have placed it in the top 10 (possibly around #5 or #6) books in my library.  And no, you can’t borrow it.  I don’t loan my books!  🙂
