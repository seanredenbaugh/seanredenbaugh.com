I am happy to announce that I have decided on an editor for my novel Sunlight Parted! I searched for weeks online and sent out several feelers and questions to prospecting editors, and I believe I have selected one of the best out there.

Her name is Cara Lockwood. She is a USA Today best-selling author of 10 books. One of her books, I Do (But I Don’t), was made into a Lifetime Original Movie starring Denise Richards and Dean Cain. You can learn more about her books at http://www.caralockwood.com/, and more about her professional editing services at http://www.edit-my-novel.com/.

I have sent her the rough draft, and since I am getting married on June 14th, we have set a date of June 25th (when I get back from the Dominican Republic), for her to return the edits to me. Here’s crossing my fingers that my writing is acceptable…
