I want to thank all my friends and readers that attended my book signing April 22nd at Barnes & Noble in Evansville, IN. I had a great turnout and sold between 25-30 books. There are still some on the shelves at the store. Please check the Local Author section if you are hunting for it. Also, thanks to the 7-8 people that I had not met before that day who bought books. It was very nice to meet you and I appreciate the support.
